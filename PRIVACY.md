# KALLOS Flick for Mac — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

KALLOS Co., Ltd. provides KALLOS Flick for Mac, an image sequence player. This policy applies to the Mac App Store version of the app.

## Data handling

Flick does not require an account. The app itself does not send personal data, selected images, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports to KALLOS or third parties. The Mac App Store version does not use the GitHub updater used by other distribution channels. Its Check updates button opens Flick's App Store page when you choose it.

When you select an image sequence, Flick reads the selected files on your Mac to display and play them. If disk proxy caching is enabled, it may create reduced-resolution copies in its local application cache to speed up repeat playback. The selected images and proxy files remain on your device. You can turn off disk proxy caching in the app.

If Flick's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is released.
